ABOUT ROIV FREIGHT COORDINATION

Helping carriers move with greater confidence.

At Roiv Freight Coordination, we help owner-operators and growing fleets operate more efficiently through dependable, results-driven dispatch services. Our focus is on keeping your trucks moving, reducing empty miles, and helping you work toward stronger profitability with freight solutions tailored to your operation.

From sourcing quality freight and negotiating competitive rates to route planning, broker communication, and back-office support, we manage the day-to-day coordination that keeps your business running smoothly. By taking care of operational details, we allow you to focus on driving, serving your customers, and growing your business with confidence.

We believe successful dispatching is built on consistency, transparency, and long-term partnerships. Whether you're an independent owner-operator or managing a fleet, we're committed to responsive support, strategic coordination, and dependable service that helps you stay competitive in today's freight market.
