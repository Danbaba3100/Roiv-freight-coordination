DISPATCH SUPPORT FROM ROIV

Coordination for the details that matter.

Dry van operations depend on consistent freight planning and clear communication. We help identify freight opportunities, discuss rates, coordinate bookings and support communication through delivery. Our aim is to help you spend less time handling dispatch details and more time focused on running your business.

What dispatch support includes

  • Freight sourcing and load matching
  • Rate negotiation
  • Route and lane planning
  • Broker communication
  • Load booking and coordination through delivery
  • Dispatch paperwork and back-office support

Roiv Freight Coordination works with independent owner-operators and fleet owners. Dispatch support is available 24/7.
